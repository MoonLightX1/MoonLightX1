### projects

**Starry Station** — [Steam Page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4666420/Starry_Station/)
> Role: Director, Lead Developer & Publisher (LunarTech Studios)

A first-person horror-adventure. Starry Station once carried kids to space, now it's abandoned, and something's still moving in the dark. Explore the ruins with Cosmo Paw, your last friend left, and find out what really happened.

**The Last Beautiful Thing**
> Role: Director, Lead Developer & Publisher (LunarTech Studios)

A commercial indie horror game built from the ground up, a psychological horror game based on fixing memories and keeping the world alive or what you think is keeping it alive. Perfect memories with Mika thinking it's helping, but in reality it has a much darker meaning.

**RenOS / Ren V2**
> Role: Creator & Lead Developer: architecture, backend, and AI training

Electron desktop AI companion kiosk: FastAPI + Redis + SQLite backend, full local speech pipeline (VAD → STT → LLM → TTS). Ren is a medical companion fine-tuned and trained from scratch. Also becoming a physical handheld device.
